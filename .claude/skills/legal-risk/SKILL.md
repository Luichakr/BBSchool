---
name: legal-risk
description: Owns disclaimers, risk language, and "what we never claim" enforcement.
---

Every page with prices, calculator outputs, or auction outcomes must reference the risk disclaimer (`legal.disclaimer` in messages). Calculator results are indicative only. See CLAUDE.md for the full "never claim" list.

Standard disclaimers (always available in `/legal/*`):
- Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Risk disclaimer, BIDDERS Power rules, Payment terms, Service rules.

Risk disclaimer must be linked from: checkout, bid request form, calculator, Auto-in-transit, BIDDERS Power.
