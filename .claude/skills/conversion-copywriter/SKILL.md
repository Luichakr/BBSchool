Tone: confident, calm, technical. Not infobiz. Not aggressive landing-page hype.

- Open every page with the value, not features.
- Address the buyer's pain ("opaque costs", "fear of bad lots", "what happens after winning") before solution.
- Never promise profit, zero risk, or direct auction access on Basic/Pro.
- CTA pairs: primary action ("Choose a package") + secondary low-commitment ("See how it works"). Avoid stacking 3+ CTAs.
